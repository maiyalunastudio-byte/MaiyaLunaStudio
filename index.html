About Me

A Little About Me

Hi, I'm the creative behind MaiyaLunaStudio. I've always been drawn to beauty, fashion, photography, and the little details that make people and their stories unique. Creating has always been one of the ways I express myself, and over time that love for creativity grew into the vision behind MaiyaLunaStudio. I created this space because I wanted somewhere that felt personal, feminine, welcoming, and inspiring — a place where people can step in front of the camera, feel confident in themselves, and create something they are genuinely proud of. I'm constantly inspired by different people, cultures, styles, places, and experiences, and I love bringing those influences together through photography and visual storytelling. For me, it's not just about taking a beautiful picture. It's about creating an experience, capturing a feeling, and giving someone a moment where they can truly see themselves in a different light. MaiyaLunaStudio is a reflection of my creativity, curiosity, and belief that everyone has something beautiful worth expressing.

About MaiyaLunaStudio

MaiyaLunaStudio is a creative photography and visual storytelling studio built around beauty, confidence, culture, and self-expression. My work explores the connection between contemporary style and personal identity, creating images that feel both artistic and authentic. From feminine portraits and fashion to swimwear, lifestyle, creative concepts, and cultural-inspired storytelling, every project is an opportunity to create something memorable. MaiyaLunaStudio is a growing creative space for women, models, artists, and collaborators who want to create something beautiful together. I'm always open to new ideas, creative concepts, and collaborations that celebrate individuality and authentic self-expression. Every woman has a story. Let's create something beautiful from yours.

My Vision

I believe photography should capture more than a beautiful image... it should capture a feeling, a personality, and a story. MaiyaLunaStudio is a space where women can feel comfortable being themselves, explore their creativity, and express their individuality without having to fit into a certain mold. Whether you're confident and bold, soft and elegant, playful and adventurous, or somewhere in between, there is room for you here.
